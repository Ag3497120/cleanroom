# Code of conduct

Cleanroom is a place to make and learn without having to prove that you belong.
This policy applies to issues, pull requests, project discussions and official project spaces.

## Expected conduct

- Be respectful, patient and specific. Critique a design or behavior, not a person's worth.
- Welcome different languages, backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Ask before sharing another person's code, private profile, learning history or identity.
- Treat self-reported experience as a person's account, not an invitation to grade them.
- Respect decisions to skip, delegate, ask for help or leave a conversation.
- Disagree with reasons and evidence. Acknowledge corrections.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, hate speech, sexual harassment, doxxing, targeted humiliation,
repeated unwanted contact, credential exposure and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.
Do not use personal learning records to shame people or rank contributors.

## Reporting and response

For a public moderation problem, open a concise issue describing the behavior and link
only to already-public material. Do not publish private messages or identifying details.
For a sensitive report, request a private contact channel from the maintainer without
posting the incident details publicly. This project does not promise an always-staffed
confidential reporting service.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, request a change in conduct, temporarily restrict
participation or ban a participant. Responses should be proportionate and protect affected
people. A participant may request clarification or reconsideration without retaliation.

This policy does not require agreement on technical decisions or disclosure of personal history.
