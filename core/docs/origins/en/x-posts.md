> Some people may already have begun to notice: those of us who develop with AI may gradually be becoming consumers rather than developers.
> 
> We think we are making something by using AI intensively, but perhaps we are handing over our thinking and merely consuming the products of large technology companies.
> 
> Perhaps the only real developers are the people uncovering the sources of intelligence at companies such as Anthropic and OpenAI, while the rest of us have become consumers of the tokens they provide.
> 
> Using Astra brought that vague unease sharply back to mind.
> 
> To remain developers, what can we do, and what will be required of us? Perhaps we need to find out in order to survive.
> 
> I have not found my answer yet.

Cleanroom does not require agreement that only AI-company researchers count as developers. It responds to a more practical concern: how to preserve judgment, understanding and experience when AI produces most of the code.
