# Discussions with AI and a corrected definition

[Japanese](../ja/ai-discussions.md) · [Sources](../README.md)

This is an edited public summary, not a full transcript or a claim that every earlier technical assertion was validated.

The first description emphasized classifying decisions before work and saving rules, checks, failures and learning candidates afterward. The author corrected that framing: the purpose is to help people remain the owners, understanders and developers of projects largely implemented by AI.

Decision rights, evidence, UNKNOWN and rule promotion are means, not the product's highest goal. Cleanroom must not make users pay an onboarding tax in unfamiliar concepts before work can begin.

## Separate the roles

- A selected work AI investigates, implements and repairs in an ordinary agent workflow.
- A selectable reflection AI proposes meanings and useful learning notes during and after work.
- Vera retains permissions, sources, execution facts, revisions, owner choices and asset states.

Do not decide meaning through keyword matching. Different models and different answers are allowed; preserve provenance. Reflection failure must not erase completed work. AI text does not become human approval, mastery or a successful test receipt.

## A research notebook with a workbench

Agent is on the left, Owner on the right. Work produces the notebook; users should not have to organize a second workload of documentation. Purpose, decisions, assumptions, bounded checks, unknowns and chosen learning sit together.

Empty Enter moves between request, memo and search. Completion inserts only an explicitly selected Owner reference. The person chooses suggestion load. Skipping is not a diagnosis of inability.

BUILD, EVIDENCE and OWNERSHIP remain separate. Building can proceed while understanding is bookmarked for another opportunity. A personal explanation is not proof that verification ran. Current guides and source describe implemented behavior; these notes describe its rationale.
